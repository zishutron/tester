Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What We Process

Zishu processes the following only when you actively use a feature that requires it:

  • Voice input — sent to our secure backend to generate AI responses
  • Text input — same purpose as voice
  • Contacts — read only when you ask Zishu to call or message a contact by name
  • Phone state — used only when placing a call via voice command
  • Location — requested only for weather, maps, or location-based queries
  • Device info — battery, network, storage, read only when you ask
  • Local storage — notes, reminders, alarms, and preferences are saved on your device only

2. How We Use It

Voice and text are sent to our backend only to generate a response. Contacts, SMS, calls, apps, settings, and location are accessed only when you issue a matching voice command. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Retention

Conversation history is stored locally on your device. Notes, reminders, and alarms are stored in your device's local storage. You can clear all of it by clearing Zishu's app data from Android Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Zishu uses a separate backend to handle AI requests so sensitive API credentials are never embedded in the client app. Network requests to third-party services (Google Maps, web search) follow their respective privacy policies.

5. Permissions

You can grant or deny each permission from Zishu Settings or Android Settings. Denying a permission disables only the features that need it — the rest of Zishu continues to work.

6. Children

Zishu is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email support.stardustofficial@gmail.com.
