About

Z

Zishu

A premium voice-first AI assistant.

The Story

Zishu was created from the idea of building a voice assistant that feels simple and natural to use. The project has been developed progressively, with continuous improvements to voice interaction, AI conversations, user experience, multilingual support, responsive design, assistant tools, accessibility, and overall reliability.

Meet the Creator
Z

Zishan

Founder & Developer · Zishu Tron
Student

Zishu is independently created and developed by Zishan, the founder and developer behind Zishu Tron. The project is being built with a focus on learning, experimentation, product development and creating useful technology.

Built by Zishu Tron

Zishu Tron is the creative technology brand behind Zishu. The project focuses on building modern digital products and experimenting with new ways technology can become more useful and accessible.

Our Vision

Technology should feel useful, simple and accessible. The long-term vision for Zishu is to continue improving the assistant so that interacting with AI becomes easier and more natural for everyday users.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Information We Process

Zishu processes voice input solely to respond to your requests. No personal data is stored without your consent.

2. How We Use Information

Voice and text inputs are sent to our secure backend to generate responses. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Retention

Conversation history is stored locally on your device. You can clear it at any time from your browser or app settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Zishu uses a separate backend to handle AI requests so sensitive API credentials are not embedded directly in the client application.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact support.stardustofficial@gmail.com.
